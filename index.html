Account Deletion for Kagami

App Developed by: Mayank Sahu

This portal allows you to permanently delete your Kagami account and all associated data.

Step 1: Re-authenticate with Google

Your account was created using Google Sign-In. For security, we require you to re-authenticate (sign in again) to confirm your request for permanent deletion.

Note: This action is irreversible once confirmed.

Step 2: FINAL CONFIRMATION

You are currently signed in as:

By clicking the button below, you confirm that you want to delete your account. This will trigger a permanent server-side process:

  • Your entire Firebase Authentication profile will be deleted.
  • All your financial transaction history and budgets in Firestore will be recursively erased.
  • This deletion is immediate and irreversible.
